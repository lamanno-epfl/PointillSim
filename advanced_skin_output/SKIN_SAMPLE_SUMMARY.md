## Tissue Architecture (8 Regions)

The tissue is organized as a vertical cross-section from the skin surface (y=0) downward to the subcutaneous fat (y=1000 µm). Each region is defined by a **Shapely polygon** (with wavy boundaries for realism) and assigned a **FOVDistribution** that controls what cell types and structures appear within it.

| # | Region | Depth (µm) | Priority | Blend Band | Polygon Type |
|---|--------|-----------|----------|------------|--------------|
| 1 | Stratum Corneum | 0–15 | 5 (top) | 5 µm | Wavy layer |
| 2 | Stratum Granulosum | 15–30 | 4 | 5 µm | Wavy layer |
| 3 | Stratum Spinosum | 30–60 | 3 | 5 µm | Wavy layer |
| 4 | Stratum Basale | 60–80 | 2 | 5 µm | Wavy layer |
| 5 | DEJ (Dermal-Epidermal Junction) | 80–100 | 2 | 15 µm | Wavy layer (rete ridges) |
| 6 | Papillary Dermis | 100–200 | 1 | 20 µm | Rectangular box |
| 7 | Reticular Dermis | 200–800 | 0 | 20 µm | Rectangular box |
| 8 | Hypodermis | 800–1000 | -1 (bottom) | 30 µm | Wavy upper boundary |

**Priority** determines which region "wins" when polygons overlap. Higher-priority regions overwrite lower ones. **Blend bands** create smooth transitions at region borders.

**Boundary waviness** (epidermal layers):
- Surface: amplitude 15 µm, 8 waves
- DEJ: amplitude 20 µm, 12 rete ridges
- Hypodermis upper boundary: amplitude 25 µm, 7 waves

## TissueSlice Assembly

Regions are assembled into a `TissueSlice` with priorities controlling overlap resolution. The `generate_global()` method:
1. Generates each region's FOV independently
2. Clips cells to each region's Shapely polygon
3. Resolves overlaps using priorities (higher priority wins)
4. Applies blend bands at region boundaries
